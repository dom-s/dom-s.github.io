Summary

Researcher working at the intersection of generative AI, natural language processing, and information retrieval. At Goldman Sachs I work on retrieval-augmented generation, embedding models, hallucination detection, and Responsible AI for financial documents, work that has led to publications at NeurIPS and IEEE Big Data and to multiple patents. Experienced across the full ML lifecycle, from experimentation and fine-tuning to production deployment, in close collaboration with engineering and product teams.
